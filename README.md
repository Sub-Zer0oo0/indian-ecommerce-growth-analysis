# indian-ecommerce-growth-analysis
Excel dashboard project analyzing pricing and revenue growth trends in Indian e‑commerce, with clear visual insights and decision support metrics.
Overview
This project analyzes financial and operational performance of an Indian e‑commerce dataset (2020–2026). The dataset includes variables such as revenue, pricing, discounts, marketing spend, website traffic, conversion rates, customer ratings, competitor price index, logistics cost, return rate, inventory levels, and festival season impact.

The goal is to build a comprehensive KPI dashboard that provides actionable insights across Sales, Pricing, Marketing, Customer Experience, and Operations.

🔑 Key Findings
1. Sales & Revenue
Steady revenue growth from 2020–2026, with sharp spikes during festival seasons.

Average Order Value (AOV) increased year‑on‑year, showing higher customer willingness to spend.

Festival seasons contributed 20–30% uplift in revenue compared to non‑festival months.

2. Pricing & Discounts
Heavy discounting boosted units sold but eroded gross margins in certain categories.

Price competitiveness index revealed that categories with aggressive pricing outperformed competitors, but at the cost of profitability.

Returns accounted for a noticeable revenue leakage, especially in electronics.

3. Marketing & Conversion
Marketing ROI varied widely — categories with targeted campaigns achieved >3x ROI, while others underperformed.

Conversion rates improved steadily, but cost per conversion was highest in fashion, indicating inefficient ad spend.

Website traffic correlated strongly with festival promotions, confirming seasonal dependence.

4. Customer Experience
Customer ratings averaged 4.1/5, with wellness and home products leading satisfaction.

Higher ratings correlated positively with units sold, validating the importance of product quality.

Return rates were highest in apparel, highlighting fit/size issues.

5. Operations & Inventory
Inventory turnover ratio improved, but stock‑out risks were frequent in fast‑moving categories.

Logistics costs consumed 8–12% of revenue, with spikes during high‑volume months.

Return logistics added significant operational burden, especially in bulky product categories.

6. Category Insights
Electronics & Fashion drove the largest revenue share but faced margin pressure.

Wellness & Home products showed consistent growth with strong customer satisfaction.

Festival season uplift was most pronounced in fashion and electronics, confirming their reliance on promotional cycles.

📈 Dashboard Features
Revenue trend lines (year/month)

Discount vs. sales scatter plots (elasticity analysis)

Conversion funnel (traffic → purchase)

Customer rating vs. sales correlation

Inventory vs. units sold bar charts (stock‑out risk)

Festival vs. non‑festival comparison blocks

✅ Takeaway:  
The dashboard provides a 360° view of business health — balancing growth, profitability, marketing efficiency, customer satisfaction, and operational resilience. It highlights where the company excels (festival sales, customer satisfaction in wellness/home) and where corrective action is needed (discount‑driven margin erosion, apparel returns, logistics cost management).
